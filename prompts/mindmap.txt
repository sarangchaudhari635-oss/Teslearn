## Photosynthesis

### What it is
- Plants make food from light
- Happens mainly in leaves
- Chloroplasts do the work

### Inputs
- Sunlight
- Carbon dioxide (CO₂)
- Water (H₂O)

### Outputs
- Glucose (sugar)
- Oxygen (O₂)

### Equation
- $6CO_2 + 6H_2O \rightarrow C_6H_12O_6 + 6O_2$
- Light energy drives the reaction

### Why it matters
- Food for the plant
- Oxygen for animals
- Base of most food chains
